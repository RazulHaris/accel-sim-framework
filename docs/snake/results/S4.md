## 6. Findings

- **Stop rule triggered.** Mode 1 lowers the demand L1 hit rate by more than 2% (relative) against Snake-DT on:
  - **hotspot**: Snake 32.3 → 26.0 (−19.6%), Snake-T 32.3 → 25.6 (−20.6%)
  - **lud**: Snake 8.4 → 8.0 (−5.1%); Snake-T is unchanged
  - IPC stays within 2% of Snake-DT everywhere (closest: histo Snake-T −1.87%, Snake −1.27%).
- **hotspot: the bulk free evicts prefetched sectors before their use (decoupling, not throttling).**
  - Its baseline hit rate is 0.2%, so its demand hits are timely prefetches: Snake-DT has 55,772 hits and 55,468 timely sectors; Snake-T has 44,301 and 44,101.
  - Evicted before use rises from 1,269 (Snake-DT) to 19,506 (Snake-T, 13.2% of issued), and all 19,506 left through bulk frees (`bulk_free_pf_unused`). Snake-T made 2,535 bulk frees, 1,590 of them on the L1 side.
  - 18,999 unused prefetched sectors lost their protection when their line flipped on the first demand hit (OQ-12, one flag per line). This roughly matches the evicted count. The likely path: after a line flips, its other prefetched sectors are L1 data, and an L1-side bulk free (25% of the set at once) removes them before their demand. The flip and bulk-free counts point to this path; the side of each eviction is not recorded.
  - Snake-DT evicted almost nothing before use (S3c): the 128 KB L1D held hotspot's prefetches without decoupling.
- **lud (Snake only): fewer prefetches.** Throttling held issue for 19.0% of cycles (17.5% bandwidth). `pf_issued` fell from 432,154 to 301,912, coverage_paper from 45.7% to 26.4% and timely sectors from 18,098 to 15,539. Demand hits fell from 46,272 to 43,902. IPC rose 0.43% against Snake-DT.
- **Throttling** (Snake, Snake-ISO): throttled 1.2–19.0% of cycles, mostly by the bandwidth trigger. The space trigger matters in hotspot (9.3%) and histo (3.5%). Against Snake-T, throttling lowers `pf_issued` by 20% (hotspot), 30% (lud), 23% (histo) and 24% (srad_v1); mri-q is unchanged. The chain cap cut 33k chain steps in histo only.
- **50% until trained (OQ-15)** holds for 33% of Snake cycles in lud, 53% in histo and 16% in srad_v1, because each kernel launch resets it on each SM. In histo it forced 40–42k demand evictions.
- **Snake-ISO** keeps hotspot's hit rate (32.5) and Snake-DT's accuracy_paper (32.3), and has the best histo IPC of all four configs (−1.01% vs the baseline). Its buffer rarely overflows (0–3,136 evictions; evicted before use ≤ 1.6% of issued).
- **Snake-DT < Snake-T ≤ Snake** (accuracy_paper) holds only for histo (8.8 / 9.2 / 9.7). In IPC it holds only for lud.
- mri-q is unaffected (2,008 issued in every config).

# S4b: revised decoupling mode 1 (2026-10-15)

S4's mode 1 contradicted §5.7: there, decoupled Snake's L1 hit rate is within ~5 points of Isolated-Snake, and decoupling raises the hit rate. S4b adds `-snake_decouple_variant`: 0 is the S4 reading, 1 the revised one and the new default (SPEC OQ-12, OQ-15):
- untrained cap ≤ 10 cycles per kernel launch
- the prefetch side of a bulk free gives only stale lines (unused for more than 50 cycles); the rest comes from the L1 side
- a line flips to the L1 region only when none of its prefetched sectors is fresh

## S4b.6 Findings

- **Step 1: the untrained cap (variant 0).**
  - The cap is re-armed only when an SM starts a kernel launch. There were 0 switches back to a launch already seen, and demotion never re-arms it (only `on_kernel_switch` clears the flag). There is no re-arm bug, so no fix commit.
  - SM-launches that do promote reach their first promotion after a p50 of 57–655 Snake cycles (max 941; histo slowest). This is far longer than the paper's "up to 10 cycles" of training.
  - Most cap time comes from SM-launches that never promote: lud 203 of 763 (93% of its cap cycles), histo 56 of 213 (94%), srad_v1 80 of 321 (79%). Those are SMs that run too few warps of a launch to see 3 warps take one edge. hotspot and mri-q always promote, and their cap is short (0.9% and 0.3% of cycles).
  - Variant 1 cuts the untrained cap to 0.1–0.3% of cycles on every app.
- **Target (§5.7) missed:** variant 1 Snake's demand L1 hit rate must be within 5 points of Snake-ISO and no more than 2% below Snake-DT.
  - **hotspot**: 26.6% vs ISO 32.5 (−6.0 pts) and DT 32.3 (−17.7%); v0 26.0.
  - **lud**: 8.2% vs DT 8.4 (−3.0%); within 0.1 pt of ISO.
  - histo (72.4 vs DT 71.8, ISO 73.3), mri-q and srad_v1 meet the target.
- **hotspot: the 50-cycle staleness rule misfires.**
  - The flip no longer exposes sectors: unused sectors evicted from L1-region lines fall from 19 to 8. But 15,608 unused sectors are still evicted from protected prefetch-region lines, through 9,693 stale lines freed.
  - Its timely inter-warp prefetches are used a p50 of 897 cycles after issue (p90 1,699; `snake_dist_lead_issue_inter_warp_timely`). A line unused for 50 cycles is therefore not dead here. §3.2's "consumed within 50 cycles" does not hold for inter-warp lookahead on our GPU.
  - Snake-T v1 is hit by the same rule (27.0%).
- **lud:** Snake-T v1 is within 1% of Snake-DT (8.3 vs 8.4); Snake v1 is 3.0% below. The rest of the gap is throttling, as in S4: issue held 18.8% of cycles, `pf_issued` 303k vs 434k.
- **histo** improves: hit rate 71.7 → 72.4 and IPC −2.85% → −1.85% vs the baseline (Snake-T −3.44% → −2.39%). Variant 1 removes histo's 53%-of-cycles cap. Deferred flips cut the sectors exposed at a flip from 95k to 63k.
- As instructed, variant 1 stays the default after this one revision; the remaining gap is recorded in SPEC §8.
