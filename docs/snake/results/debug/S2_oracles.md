# Debug plan Stage 2: upper bounds for Snake in our environment (2026-10-29)

**Status:** done. 18 runs in `sim_run_snake_debug/s1s2` (19:41-20:48, all exit 0), build accel-sim `bc03386` + gpgpu-sim `37846351`. Flags `-snake_oracle_time` and `-snake_oracle_pred` (tokens `ORACLE_TIME`, `ORACLE_PRED`) default off; gates DBG1 passed (S1_environment.md).

## TLDR

- **Gate G2 fails on both counts.**
  - **O-TIME** (every issued prefetch filled at once): −0.5% GMEAN vs Tier 1's −0.3%. Largest per-workload gap +0.3 points. Timeliness fixes are not worth pursuing.
  - **O-PRED** (every predicted sector installed at once): +1.5% GMEAN, +2.0 points over O-TIME (the bar is 3). Issue-loss fixes are not worth pursuing.
  - **Per the plan:** Snake's own predictions cannot produce large gains in this environment. Stages 3-5 only rule out bugs, Stage 6 skips the extensions, and the write-up states the conclusion.
- **Hit rate is not the problem; useful coverage is.**
  - O-TIME raises the L1 hit rate a lot and IPC not at all: backprop 42 → 58%, srad_v1 32 → 42%, lud 512 7 → 32%, hotspot 1024 28 → 67%.
  - Only near-complete coverage pays: O-PRED's backprop at 90% hit gives +11.4% (perfect L1 +41.7%), srad_v1 at 95% gives +3.5% (+20.6%).
- **Where Snake predicts well, there is nothing to gain.** hotspot, lud and mri-q have 76-93% of demand sectors timely under O-PRED and gain at most +1.0%, because their perfect-L1 ceilings are +0.1 to +3.6% (Stage 1).
- **Where the headroom is, Snake has little or harms:**
  - nw: no prediction at all.
  - histo: O-TIME −3.5%, O-PRED −5.1% despite a higher hit rate (Stage 3 looks at why).
  - backprop and srad_v1: only O-PRED's full coverage helps.
- **O-PRED is generous.** Many memory copies of its installs were dropped because the miss queue was full (backprop 39%, srad_v1 46%, mri-q 75% of copies). It therefore under-counts the traffic its prefetches would cost, and is an upper bound in that sense too.
