## TLDR

- **Gate G1: the decoder is not the gap.**
  - With the bug-compatible decoder, the 7-app means barely move: baseline L1 hit 18.9% (corrected 18.7%), reservation fails 21.4% (21.2%).
  - Tier 1 Snake is −0.49% GMEAN (corrected −0.29%), and the perfect-L1 ceiling is +16.61% (+16.62%).
  - Only backprop and hotspot have mis-decoded lines: 172,032 on backprop (all in its first kernel), 1,673 on hotspot 512. Backprop's baseline L1 hit moves 41.7 → 42.8%, hotspot's by under 1 point.
  - At 100M the same lines moved backprop from 13.4% to 36.2% (VERSION_DIFF 8a). Over the whole run, the L1 reads they change are 2.6% of all (25,130 of 999,993).
  - Nothing moves toward the paper. Neutral wording: the decoder in the release the paper names reads these traces differently; with it, our Tier 1 Snake gets −0.49% GMEAN (−0.29% corrected).
- **So H1 holds (our baseline is less memory-bound), but neither H1a (decoder) nor H1b (config) explains it.**
  - Remaining candidates, not testable with the plan's runs: the paper traced the apps itself (other CUDA or inputs), or v1.2.0 code differences outside the options (VERSION_DIFF).

- **Our corrected baseline is much less memory-bound than the paper's.** 7-app means, ours vs the paper's per-app values:
  - L1 hit: 18.7 vs 31.3%.
  - Reservation fails (Fig. 3): 21.2 vs 39.4%.
  - L1-L2 reply bandwidth used (Fig. 4): 11.6 vs 31.3%.
  - All warps on the scoreboard (Fig. 5 proxy): 39.0 vs 57.1%.
- **The gap is largest on hotspot, lud and mri-q.** backprop and histo are memory-bound in ours too (perfect-L1 ceilings +42 and +39%).
  - hotspot: 5-8% reservation fails vs 57%; perfect-L1 ceiling +0.1-0.3%.
  - lud and mri-q: 0.5-2% L1-L2 bandwidth vs 20-22%; perfect-L1 ceiling +2-4%.
  - The paper gets +13% (hotspot), +18% (lud) and +4% (mri-q) from Snake on these. Even a perfect L1 gives ours almost nothing.
- **Config (1.2): no option difference explains it.**
  - Ours equals v1.2.0-era dev and Table 1 on the memory system.
  - The release tag's L1 (32 KB streaming, 4096 MSHRs) contradicts Table 1, and would give fewer reservation fails, not more.
  - 14 options differ. The L1 options are ours = Table 1's. The 1530 MHz clock is also Table 1's, and makes ours slightly *more* memory-bound.
  - Two others could matter. The perfect instruction/constant cache (ours on, v1.2.0 off) is a "maybe", with mri-q the most exposed. v1.2.0's longer ALU latencies point the wrong way.
  - The rest have no effect on our workloads.
- **The precise Fig. 5 counter** (no warp issued and every scoreboard-blocked warp waits on a memory register) is far below the proxy at 100M:
  - hotspot: 0% vs 4-16%. Its scoreboard waits are on shared-memory and ALU registers.
  - histo: 56 vs 88%.
  - At 1B the bug-compatible baselines carry the counter (7-app mean 21.4% vs the paper's 57%). A corrected-decoder 1B value would need 9 more runs, which are not in the plan's budget and were not run. Corrected and bug-compatible agree on 5 of 7 apps anyway.
