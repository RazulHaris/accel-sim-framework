## 1. Definitions

- **Prediction record.** One per predicted sector, the OQ-14 window entry (`snake_pred_sectors` records). A re-prediction of a pending sector updates the record's cycle and mode, so a record's mode is that of its **latest** prediction. Its state is the result of the latest attempt (queued, dropped, duplicate), except that once issued it stays issued.
- **Funnel classes.** A record ends in exactly one class (`snake_funnel_invariant`):
  - **Matched** (a demand sector within 5,000 cycles, the coverage_pred numerator), classified at that demand:
    - still queued
    - dropped: prefetch queue full (incl. displaced, OQ-31)
    - dropped: miss queue (`pf_drop_miss_queue`, 98% of it from the reserve test, `pf_drop_mq_reserve`)
    - dropped: line or MSHR reserve
    - duplicate: in L1, or an MSHR entry, at offer
    - issued: late (the demand merged), evicted or invalidated before use, timely (the demand hit), other
  - **Wrong:** no demand within the window; split into issued / not issued.
  - **Live:** still within the window at the end of the run.
  - A prediction that merges into a sector already pending in the queue (`pf_dup_queue`) is the same record, so it takes that entry's fate; it is not a class of its own.
- **Lead time** (`lead_issue_*`) is per issued sector: issue → first demand load, by the mode of the queue entry that issued it. The per-mode funnel (§3) uses the record's latest mode instead. A sector that an inter-warp entry issued and the warp's own chain predicted again later therefore counts as inter-thread in §3 but as inter-warp in §4 (hotspot: the inter-thread records are 42% timely, but no inter-thread-issued sector was timely).
- **Miss-queue wait** = push → sent to the interconnect, per request.

## 2. Miss-queue drain (from the code)

- `baseline_cache::cycle()` (`gpu-cache.cc`) sends **at most one request per core cycle**: the head, if `m_memport->full(size, write)` is false. There is no other path out of the queue.
- For the L1D, `full()` is `shader_memory_interface::full` → `simt_core_cluster::icnt_injection_buffer_full` (`shader.cc:4684`) → `!icnt_has_buffer(cluster, size)`.
- The config uses `-network_mode 2` (local xbar, `local_interconnect.cc`). `LocalInterconnect::HasBuffer` ignores the packet size and tests the SM's request-network input buffer: `in_buffers[sm].size() + 1 <= icnt_in_buffer_limit` (512 packets). Out buffers are 512 as well; the xbar moves at most one packet per input and per output each icnt cycle (iSLIP); the icnt clock is the core clock (1530 MHz).
- **Measured:** the input buffer never refused the head in any run (0 blocked cycles), and every non-empty cycle sent exactly one request. The miss queue is limited by its **one-per-cycle drain**, not by the interconnect.
- The L1D miss queue has 16 entries. A demand read miss needs `size + 1 < 16`; a prefetch needs `size + 1 + reserve ≤ 16`, i.e. at most 7 entries queued with reserve 8, and at most 11 with reserve 4.

## 3. §3.2 ¶4: prefetching for other warps at promotion

- **Yes, once.** `train_inter_thread` → `promoted_predictions` (OQ-27). At the observe where an entry reaches promoted (3 warps in its vector), every other warp whose Head register holds PC1 gets a `promotion` prediction for PC2 (Head address + stride). The warp must not have its bit set and must not have exited. Fig. 15 (b)'s 1800 is this case.
- **Not afterwards.** A warp that reaches PC1 later without its bit gets no inter-thread prediction: the own-warp chain (`predict`, `best_entry`) requires the warp's bit even at depth 1. Such a warp is covered only through the inter-warp targets and their chains (OQ-29, which do not need the bit), or after it takes the edge itself.
- **How often** (`snake_promoted_nobit_loads`, §7 below): loads at a promoted PC1 by a warp without its bit are 15% of observed loads in hotspot, 42% in lud, 57% in mri-q, 37% in srad_v1 and 0.8% in histo.

## 10. Findings

- **Where predictions end** (current settings, % of predicted sectors):

  | app | wrong | matched | of which issued late / timely | never issued in time (queued + q-full + miss-queue) | dup L1 | live |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | hotspot 512 | 9.9 | 66.0 | 28.6 / 24.8 | 12.4 | 0.1 | 24.1 |
  | srad_v1 | 12.4 | 81.0 | 21.5 / 7.8 | 35.0 | 16.7 | 6.6 |
  | mri-q | 35.7 | 64.3 | 6.9 / 0.1 | 57.5 | 0.0 | 0.0 |
  | lud 512 | 53.8 | 40.1 | 19.5 / 1.5 | 17.9 | 1.3 | 6.1 |
  | histo | 40.7 | 42.9 | 21.4 / 13.2 | 6.9 | 1.2 | 16.4 |

  Nothing issued is evicted before use (≤ 0.01%), and line/MSHR drops are 0. The losses are prefetches issued too late, or never issued (still queued, dropped with the queue full, or dropped at the miss queue). hotspot's 24% `live` reflects its short run (19.7k cycles against the 5,000-cycle window).
- **Wrong predictions** come from promotion and the inter-warp chain: lud 79% of promotion records and 87% of inter-warp-chain records, histo 46% of inter-warp records. lud's are mostly not issued (38.1 of its 53.8%).
- **mri-q never gets its predictions out:** queue wait p50 113 cycles (p90 314), and 31% of predicted sectors are still queued when the demand arrives. Its demand misses keep the miss queue near full (demand requests wait 15 cycles at p50, about one full queue), so prefetches rarely find the 8 free slots the reserve needs.
- **Lead times** (issue → first demand, p50): inter-thread prefetches arrive late after ~20 cycles; inter-warp ones after 38–119 cycles when late, or 178–1,075 cycles when timely. Late prefetches therefore start one L1-latency ahead, not a memory latency ahead. histo's late inter-thread prefetches wait 437 cycles, which points to long memory queues.
- **Prediction → issue** p50 is 3–11 cycles (inter-thread, inter-warp) and 8–43 cycles (inter-warp chain); the queue wait is 10–19 cycles except in mri-q. There is no reserve wait: a sector refused by the reserve is dropped, never retried (OQ-11). The port wait is 0.
- **Miss queue:** demand requests wait 6.5–7.3 cycles (mri-q 14.6), prefetches 4.5–5.9. 6–34% of demand pushes find a prefetch ahead of them, and 2.5–13.6% of non-empty cycles send a prefetch while a demand waits behind it.
- **histo's prediction gap is real:** coverage_pred is 30.9% with the exact footprint (27.1% with the line footprint, S3b), 41% of its predicted sectors match no demand, and its baseline L1 hit rate is 63%. dIPC is −1.8% (line: −3.8%).
- **Reserve 4 vs 8:**
  - Miss-queue drops fall by 26–32% (mri-q 10%) and coverage_paper rises by 1.7–4.0 pp: hotspot 71.1 → 75.1, lud 43.3 → 46.0, mri-q 9.5 → 13.4, srad_v1 29.2 → 30.9.
  - Prefetch-queue-full drops do not change.
  - Demand pays: its miss-queue wait rises (hotspot 7.2 → 8.0, lud 6.5 → 7.8 cycles), and demand MISS_QUEUE_FULL rises (hotspot 9,181 → 13,177, srad_v1 39,609 → 47,035, lud 23,751 → 53,605, i.e. +25% over the baseline's 42,855).
  - IPC: hotspot −0.15 → +0.28%, lud −0.26 → −0.57%, mri-q +0.16 → +0.11%, srad_v1 unchanged (+0.3%).
  - Reserve 4 trades demand latency for coverage; it does not fix lateness.
